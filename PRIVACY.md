# Pidgy Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

Pidgy creates pixel-art wallpapers and matching Home Screen widgets. The app works on your device and does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or backend server.

## Information processed on your device

When you choose a Home Screen screenshot with the system Photos picker, Pidgy processes that image locally to measure widget placement and match the wallpaper. The app does not upload the screenshot to the developer or a third party.

Scene preferences, device display information, and alignment measurements are stored in the app’s on-device storage, shared with its widget extension. This data is used to render your chosen scene. Removing the app removes its local storage, subject to Apple’s normal backup and restore behavior.

## Photos permission

Pidgy requests permission to add generated wallpaper images to Photos. Selecting a screenshot uses the system Photos picker; the app does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. Wallpaper images saved to Photos remain there until you delete them in Photos.

## Data collection and tracking

Pidgy does not collect data from your device for the developer, track you across apps or websites, or sell personal information. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this release.

Apple may separately collect purchase, download, or diagnostic information according to your Apple account and device settings. Those services are governed by Apple’s policies.

## Support

Contact the developer through [Pidgy’s support tracker](https://github.com/agamatlab/Pidgy/issues). The tracker is provided by GitHub and has its own privacy policy. Support posts are public: do not include private screenshots, passwords, or other sensitive information.

## Changes

If Pidgy’s data practices change, this policy will be updated for the relevant app version.
